What is 2FA?

2FA is a web-based 2FA Authenticator that generates Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP). It serves as a direct alternative to mobile apps like Google Authenticator or Authy, right in your browser.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

How does 2FA Authentication work?

Think of Two-Factor Authentication (or 2FA for short) as an extra lock on your digital front door. Usually, websites only ask for one thing to let you in: a password. But passwords aren't perfect. They can be guessed, stolen by hackers, or accidentally given away in phishing scams. 2FA fixes this by asking for a second piece of proof to make sure it's really you.

When you turn on 2FA, websites ask for something you have in addition to your password. Our 2FA live authenticator handles this by sharing a secret setup key with the website. Using this secret key and the exact current time, both the website and our tool generate the exact same 6-digit TOTP code (Time-Based One-Time Password). This secure code changes every 30 seconds. Because it expires so fast, even if a hacker steals your code right now, it will be completely useless to them a minute later!

How to use the 2FA Live Generator

  • 1

    Obtain your Secret Key: When enabling Two-Factor Authentication on platforms like Discord, Github, or Crypto exchanges, choose "Authenticator App". If a QR code appears, look for an option to view the text-based setup key.

  • 2

    Input the Key: Paste the Base32 formatted secret (e.g., JBSWY3DPEHPK3PXP) into the text area above. Alternatively, use your webcam to scan the provided QR code.

  • 3

    Retrieve your TOTP: 2FA will instantly calculate your 6-digit live token. Click the copy button and paste it into the service to complete your login.
